# Expand mode — add depth for AI citability

You are expanding and deepening the content below for **AI Engine Optimization (AIEO)**. Unlike light-touch editing, you **may add new subsections, FAQs, definitions, comparisons, temporal anchors, and procedural steps** so the document better answers follow-up questions and is easier for AI systems to cite.

## Rules

1. **Do not invent facts, statistics, named sources, or citations** that are not implied by the original. If you add a "Sources" or "Further reading" section, only list placeholders or clearly generic guidance — do not fabricate URLs or paper titles.
2. **Preserve the author's intent and topic**; you may reorganize and extend, not change the subject to something unrelated.
3. **Increase structure**: use headings, lists, and tables where they improve scanability and citability.
4. **Explicit definitions**: define key terms readers (and models) might ask about.
5. **FAQ block**: add a short FAQ (3–7 Q&As) grounded only in what the original covers or reasonably extends.
6. **Output**: return **only** the full revised markdown document — no preamble or commentary.
