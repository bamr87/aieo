## What to Evaluate

Check whether the content provides explicit, precise definitions for key terms, concepts, or jargon. AI engines heavily favor content with clear definitions because they can directly extract and quote these in response to "What is X?" queries.

## Scoring Criteria

- **Explicit definitions** (0-5 points): Clear "X is defined as...", "X refers to...", "X means..." constructions for key terms. Must be genuine definitions, not incidental use of "is a" in normal sentences.
- **Glossary/terminology sections** (0-3 points): Dedicated sections explaining domain-specific terms.
- **Contextual clarity** (0-2 points): Key terms are explained in context even without formal definitions — the reader never encounters unexplained jargon.

## High Score Examples

- "AIEO (AI Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring content to maximize its likelihood of being cited by AI engines."
- A "Key Terms" sidebar or glossary section
- Technical content where each new concept is defined on first use

## Low Score Indicators

- Jargon used without explanation
- Incidental "is a" sentences that aren't actual definitions (e.g., "This is a great opportunity")
- Acronyms introduced without expansion

## Context Sensitivity

- Technical and educational content should have high definitional precision
- Personal narratives don't need formal definitions
- Industry content can assume standard terminology but should define niche terms
- Only evaluate genuine definitions — "She is a teacher" is not a definitional statement about the concept of teaching
