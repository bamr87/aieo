## What to Evaluate

Determine whether the content includes FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) formatting — explicit question-answer pairs that AI engines can directly match to user queries. FAQ-structured content is among the most citable because AI engines can map user questions directly to content answers.

## Scoring Criteria

- **Dedicated FAQ section** (0-8 points): An explicit "Frequently Asked Questions", "FAQ", or "Common Questions" section with Q&A pairs. Award more for questions that reflect real user queries (not marketing fluff questions like "Why are we the best?").
- **Question headers** (0-4 points): H2/H3 headers phrased as questions (e.g., "How much does it cost?", "What browsers are supported?"). These are strong citation hooks even outside a formal FAQ.
- **Q&A density** (0-3 points): Ratio of genuine questions answered throughout the content — not just in a FAQ section.

## High Score Examples

- Dedicated FAQ section: "Q: How long does shipping take? A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days..."
- Article with question headers: "## What is AIEO?", "## How Does AIEO Differ from SEO?", "## Should I Optimize for AI Engines?"
- Service page with "Common Questions" addressing real concerns

## Low Score Indicators

- No questions anywhere in the content
- Only rhetorical questions that aren't answered
- "FAQ" section with self-serving questions ("Q: Why should I choose us?" "A: Because we're the best!")

## Context Sensitivity

- FAQ sections are most valuable on service pages, product pages, and documentation
- Blog posts benefit from question-style headers even without formal FAQ sections
- Academic or technical content naturally uses "Research Questions" — count these similarly
- Award partial credit for question-driven structure even without explicit "FAQ" label
