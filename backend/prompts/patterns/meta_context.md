## What to Evaluate

Assess whether the content explains WHY information matters — providing the broader context, significance, and implications that help AI engines understand the importance and relevance of the content for various queries.

## Scoring Criteria

- **Importance framing** (0-4 points): Explicit statements about why the topic matters, who it affects, and what's at stake. "This matters because...", "The significance of X is...", "Understanding X is critical for..."
- **Audience context** (0-3 points): Clear identification of who this content is for and why they should care. "If you're a small business owner...", "For developers working with..."
- **Broader implications** (0-3 points): Connecting specific details to larger trends, consequences, or applications. "This shift means that...", "The impact extends to..."

## High Score Examples

- "Understanding AIEO is crucial for any content creator because AI engines now drive 30% of web discovery traffic. Without optimization, your content becomes invisible."
- "This tax change affects all sole proprietors earning over $50,000 annually — approximately 12 million Americans."

## Low Score Indicators

- Content presents facts without context about why they matter
- No indication of target audience
- Missing "so what?" framing — reader can't tell why they should care

## Context Sensitivity

- Some content types naturally have strong meta-context (opinion pieces, analysis)
- Reference/documentation content may score lower here — that's acceptable
- Landing pages should always explain why the visitor should care
- Don't confuse generic importance padding ("This is very important!") with genuine contextual framing
