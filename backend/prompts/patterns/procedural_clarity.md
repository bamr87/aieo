## What to Evaluate

Assess whether the content provides clear step-by-step procedures, instructions, or processes. AI engines cite procedural content heavily because "how to" queries are among the most common — and structured procedures can be directly extracted and presented.

## Scoring Criteria

- **Numbered steps** (0-2 points): Explicit step-by-step sequences with numbered or ordered items (Step 1, Step 2..., or ordered lists).
- **Process descriptions** (0-2 points): Clear process flows even if not formally numbered — "First... then... finally..."
- **Prerequisites/outcomes** (0-1 point): States what's needed before starting and what the result will be.

## High Score Examples

- "How to Set Up Your Account: Step 1: Visit... Step 2: Click... Step 3: Enter..."
- A recipe with ingredients list, numbered steps, and expected outcome
- Installation guide with prerequisites, steps, and verification

## Low Score Indicators

- Describes a process in paragraph form without clear sequence markers
- References "easy setup" without explaining the actual steps
- Instructions buried in dense prose

## Context Sensitivity

- Not all content is procedural — opinion pieces, news, and about pages score 0 here without penalty
- Any content describing "how to" achieve something should include clear procedures
- Service pages describing "our process" benefit from structured steps
