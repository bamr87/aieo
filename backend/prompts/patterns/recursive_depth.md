## What to Evaluate

Assess whether the content anticipates and answers follow-up questions — creating the layered depth that AI engines need to satisfy multi-turn conversations. Content with recursive depth gets cited across entire conversation threads, not just the initial query.

## Scoring Criteria

- **Explicit questions answered** (0-5 points): Questions posed and answered in the content (FAQ format, question headers like "What happens if...?", "How does this compare to...?")
- **Implicit follow-ups addressed** (0-5 points): Content that naturally addresses the "but what about..." and "how exactly..." follow-ups a reader would have
- **Nested detail levels** (0-5 points): Content that provides both overview and deep-dive — summary → details → edge cases

## High Score Examples

- A "How to file taxes" article that covers: what forms you need → step-by-step instructions → what if you made a mistake → deadlines → penalties → common edge cases
- FAQ section with genuine follow-up questions, not just restated top-level questions
- Headers structured as a natural question chain: "What is X?" → "How does X work?" → "When should you use X?" → "What are alternatives to X?"

## Low Score Indicators

- Content answers one question and stops
- FAQ section with trivially obvious questions
- No question headers or Q&A formatting
- No progression from basic to advanced topics

## Context Sensitivity

- Landing pages may not need deep recursion — award partial credit for addressing the "why should I care?" follow-up
- Tutorial content should score high here — it must anticipate "what if it doesn't work?" scenarios
- Service pages benefit from "Who is this for?", "How does pricing work?", "What's the process?" progression
